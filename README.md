Managing academic progress in a Bachelor of Science in Information Technology (BSIT) program can be challenging, especially when dealing with complex prerequisite chains spanning multiple semesters. Students often struggle to track which subjects they have passed, which are currently available to them, and which remain locked due to unmet prerequisites. This frequently leads to misenrollment, academic delays, and added stress.

SubjectTracker was developed to address this problem by providing BSIT students of the University of San Carlos with a dedicated mobile application that visualizes their curriculum, tracks subject statuses, and clearly maps prerequisite relationships. The application aims to empower students to make informed enrollment decisions, monitor their academic progress at a glance, and plan their remaining semesters more effectively.

By digitizing the USC BSIT curriculum (Effective 2023) into an interactive and intuitive interface, SubjectTracker reduces reliance on manual tracking methods such as spreadsheets or physical checklists, and replaces them with a modern, mobile-first solution designed specifically for the needs of IT students.

SubjectTracker is a mobile application built using React Native and Expo, designed for BSIT students at the University of San Carlos. The application covers the complete USC BSIT curriculum effective 2023, encompassing all subjects across Years 1 through 3, including first and second semesters and summer terms. User accounts are managed through Supabase, allowing each student's progress to be securely stored and persisted across sessions.
